# Kural — YC Product Demo Script (User-Perspective Narrated)

**Purpose:** Screen recording script for the YC application demo URL. Narrated entirely from the **user's perspective** — first-person, as if the viewer is experiencing the app.

**Target length:** 2–3 minutes

**Tone:** Casual, confident, conversational. No jargon. Focus on "what this does for me" not "how it works."

---

## Product Snapshot (from codebase)

- **Name:** Kural
- **Tagline:** Value Over Virality / Value Driven Social Media
- **Core promise:** The first platform that measures **Insight**, **Rigor**, and **Utility**. Replaced the popularity contest with a value system.
- **Key differentiators:**
  - No likes → Bookmarks (save what matters)
  - Value scoring on every post (epistemic, insight, practical, relational, effort)
  - Fact-checking for factual claims (clean / needs_review / blocked)
  - "Why this post" explanations in the Curated Kurals feed
  - Natural language feed tuning ("Show me more diverse content")
  - Audience targeting when posting (describe who should see it)
  - Kurral Score for creators (quality-based reputation, monetization at ≥77)

---

## Demo Flow — What to Record (Screen-by-Screen)

### 1. Open on the main feed (0:00–0:12)

**Screen:** Kural app, main feed visible (Friends or Curated Kurals tab).

**Narration (user voice):**
> "This is Kural — my feed, but it's built differently. No likes. No chasing virality. Everything here is ranked by value, not popularity."

**What to show:** Feed with posts visible. Tabs: Friends | Curated Kurals.

---

### 2. Curated Kurals + "Why this post" (0:12–0:40)

**Screen:** Switch to **Curated Kurals** tab. Scroll to show 1–2 posts that have the **"Why this post:"** explanation box above them.

**Narration:**
> "In Curated Kurals, every post tells me why it's here. 'Matches your interest in productivity' or 'From someone you follow, strong discussion.' No black box. The algorithm explains itself."

**What to show:**
- The small box above each post: `Why this post: [explanation]`
- At least one post from someone the user follows, one from discovery

**Critical:** This is a **unique** differentiator. No other feed explains why each post was chosen.

---

### 3. Tuning the feed in plain English (0:40–1:00)

**Screen:** Open **Settings → Feed** (For You Settings), or show the For You tuning controls if visible. Use a **Smart Preset** or type a natural language instruction.

**Narration:**
> "I don't tweak sliders. I tell it what I want. 'Show me more diverse content' or 'Focus on people I follow.' It understands and adapts."

**What to show:**
- Click "Discovery" or "Stay Connected" preset, OR
- Type: "Show me more posts about startups and less about politics" → Submit → See confirmation
- Presets: Discovery | Stay Connected | Lively Discussions | Balanced

---

### 4. Reading a post — fact-check, no likes, bookmark (1:00–1:25)

**Screen:** Click into a **single post** (post detail view). Preferably one with fact-check status and/or value score.

**Narration:**
> "When I open a post, I see what matters. Fact-check status — green if clean, yellow if it needs review. No like button. I bookmark what I want to save. The platform measures insight, rigor, and utility — not how many people clicked a heart."

**What to show:**
- Fact-check badge (✓ clean / ⚠ needs_review / ✗ blocked) — top-right of post card
- Value score badge (if viewing own post): e.g. "85 value"
- Action bar: Reply, Repost, Bookmark — **no Like**
- Comments / discussion

---

### 5. Creating a post — audience targeting (1:25–1:55)

**Screen:** Open **Composer** (new post). Write a short post. Use **Audience Description** or **Reach** options.

**Narration:**
> "When I post, I choose who sees it. I pick a topic. I can describe my audience — 'people interested in productivity and building in public' — and the system matches my content to the right readers. Or I set reach: everyone, or just followers. I'm in control."

**What to show:**
- Composer open
- Type: "Three lessons from shipping our first product: 1) Ship small. 2) Talk to users early. 3) Iterate fast."
- Pick topic: e.g. #startups or #productivity
- Show **Reach** toggle: For All vs Tuned
- If Tuned: Audience Description box — type "founders and indie hackers"
- Post

---

### 6. Creator dashboard — Kurral Score (1:55–2:20)

**Screen:** Navigate to **Dashboard** (or Profile → Dashboard). Show Kurral Score, value stats, monetization status.

**Narration:**
> "As a creator, I'm recognized for quality. My Kurral Score — based on what I post and how it lands. Value I've created, not views. Monetization kicks in when I hit the bar. It's built for people who want to be known for substance, not hype."

**What to show:**
- Kurral Score (0–100) with tier (Excellent / Good / Fair / etc.)
- Value stats: post value, comment value, total value
- Monetization: Eligible / Not Eligible (score ≥77, account ≥30 days)
- Content performance: high / medium / low value posts

---

### 7. Closing — back to feed (2:20–2:40)

**Screen:** Return to main feed. Optional: show Bookmarks page briefly.

**Narration:**
> "Value over virality. Control your feed. Get recognized for what you create. That's Kural."

**What to show:**
- Feed one more time
- Optional: Bookmarks — "What I saved. What actually mattered to me."

## Recording Checklist

### Before recording
- [ ] Use a demo account with: interests, following, some posts, some bookmarks
- [ ] Ensure at least 2–3 posts in Curated Kurals have "Why this post" explanations
- [ ] Have at least one post with fact-check status (clean preferred)
- [ ] Close other tabs/apps; enable Do Not Disturb
- [ ] Browser zoom ~125% for readability
- [ ] Use incognito or clean session if needed

### During recording
- [ ] Smooth, deliberate cursor movement
- [ ] Pause 1–2 seconds on key UI elements (Why this post, fact-check badge, Kurral Score)
- [ ] Don't rush; 2–3 minutes is fine
- [ ] Audio: clear mic, quiet room

### After recording
- [ ] Trim dead air at start/end
- [ ] Upload to YouTube (unlisted) or host elsewhere
- [ ] Ensure URL is public, no password
- [ ] Paste URL in YC application: "If you have a demo, what's the url?"

## Technical Notes (from codebase)

- **Feed tabs:** Friends (latest from following) | Curated Kurals (For You)
- **For You controls:** Settings → Feed, or ForYouControls with Smart Presets + instruction
- **ChirpCard:** Reply, Repost/Quote, Bookmark — no Like
- **Value score:** Shown to author only (`isCurrentUser`)
- **Fact-check:** `clean` | `needs_review` | `blocked`
- **Composer:** Topics, reach (forAll / tuned), AudienceDescriptionBox for tuned
- **Dashboard:** Kurral Score, value stats, monetization eligibility
