# YC Founder Video — Bullet Points (1 minute)

**Goal (per YC):** Introduce yourself, explain what you’re doing and why. **Only you talking. 1 minute. No demo.**  
**Important:** Use these as **bullet points** and talk like you would to a friend. Don’t read it word-for-word.

---

## The 60-second take (recommended)

### [0:00–0:10] Who you are
- Hi, I’m Ronny — I’m the solo founder of **Kural**.

### [0:10–0:25] What you’re building (microblogging, one sentence)
- Kural is a **microblogging** app where the feed is optimized for **value**, not engagement bait.

### [0:25–0:40] Why (the insight / motivation)
- Microblogging today rewards the loudest, fastest, and most polarizing posts.
- I want a feed where **substance wins**: useful, rigorous, insightful writing actually rises.

### [0:40–0:55] The key “how” (agentic end-to-end, said simply)
- Kural is **AI-agent driven end-to-end**.
- When someone posts, agents **decide what needs verification**, **extract factual claims**, **fact-check using web sources**, then **score the post** on quality dimensions and generate the explanation.
- That same system ranks the feed and shows **“why this post”** so it isn’t a black box.

### [0:55–1:00] Close
- I’m building Kural because I think microblogging should reward **truth and usefulness**, not addiction mechanics.

## Optional swaps (use ONE, only if true)

### If you have traction numbers (best opening)
Replace the first two bullets with:
- Hi, I’m Ronny — I’m building Kural.
- We have **[X users]** and **[Y]** posts/bookmarks in **[Z weeks]**. It’s a microblogging app where the feed is optimized for value, not engagement bait.

### If you want a stronger personal “why”
Add one sentence after “Microblogging today…”:
- I kept seeing high-effort, thoughtful posts get buried, and I wanted to build the opposite incentive system.

### If you want the “why now”
Add one sentence near the end:
- With modern LLMs and retrieval, we can finally automate quality signals and make the ranking transparent.

## Quick recording checklist
- **Look**: eye contact with the camera, simple background, good light
- **Sound**: quiet room; audio clarity > video quality
- **Delivery**: one breath per bullet, no jargon, one minute, 3–5 takes, pick the most natural
